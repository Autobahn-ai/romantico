## 1. Executive summary

### The business

A specialized consulting practice that **automates manual office processes** for German trade businesses (Handwerksbetriebe) with 10–40 employees — using **low/no-code tools** (n8n, Google Sheets, Looker Studio) instead of custom software development.

### The problem

Thousands of profitable Handwerk businesses still run core office workflows through **Excel, WhatsApp, paper, and phone calls**. This creates:

- 5–15 hours/week of duplicate data entry
- Late invoicing and cash-flow delays
- No visibility on job profitability
- Owner burnout (weekend paperwork)
- Bürokraft frustration and turnover

Existing solutions fail them because:

- ERP/Handwerkersoftware is expensive and slow to implement
- Generic IT support fixes computers, not processes
- Agencies sell marketing or custom code, not practical automation
- Nobody offers a **fixed-price, 14-day, one-process fix**

### The solution

**Productized Automation Sprints** — diagnose one painful process, automate it in 14 days, hand over with documentation and 14-day support. Upsell to monthly **Care Plan** retainers.

### Financial targets (24 months)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Cumulative gross revenue | €140,000 – €172,000 |
| Cumulative net (pre-tax personal) | €87,000 – €110,000 |
| Steady-state net income (month 24) | €5,500 – €7,000/month |
| Retainer clients (month 24) | 2–3 @ €600–1,000/month |

### Investment required

| Monthly fixed costs (operating) | €80 – €150/month |
| Emergency runway before quitting job | €9,000 – €15,000 savings |

## 4. Market analysis

### Market size (Germany)

- **5.4+ million** people employed in Handwerk (ZDH data)
- **~1 million** Handwerk businesses, vast majority < 50 employees
- Digital maturity: **low** — only fraction use integrated Handwerkersoftware consistently
- Your serviceable market: businesses in **[your Bundesland/region]** with 10–40 MA struggling with Excel/WhatsApp workflows

**Conservative serviceable obtainable market (year 1–2):**

- 500 eligible businesses within 1-hour travel radius
- Target: **8–15 clients in 24 months** = 1.6–3% penetration
- Fully achievable for solo operator

### Industry trends (tailwinds)

1. **Fachkräftemangel** — offices understaffed; automation saves Bürokraft hours
2. **Digitalisierung pressure** — Handwerkskammer, funding programs (go-digital, etc.)
3. **Generational transition** — younger Inhaber more open to tools
4. **Rising labor costs** — €5k automation pays back if saves 5h/week
5. **Low-code maturity** — n8n/Airtable viable without developers

### Competition analysis

| Competitor type | Strength | Weakness | Your advantage |
|-----------------|----------|----------|----------------|
| Handwerkersoftware (ToolTime, plancraft, Hero) | Full solution | Expensive, long onboarding, overkill for one problem | Faster, cheaper, one process |
| Local IT (PC/Netzwerk) | Trust, local | No process expertise | Process-first |
| Freelance Excel help | Cheap | Unreliable, no automation | Professional delivery |
| Generic automation consultants | Technical | Don't know Handwerk | Niche fluency |
| DIY (owner/nephew) | Free | Breaks, no documentation | Done-for-you |

**Saturation verdict:** General automation is saturated; **Handwerk-specific 14-day Sprints are not** in most regions.

### Buyer behavior

- Decisions often by **Inhaber** (owner) or **Geschäftsführer**
- Trust-driven: referral > cold outreach
- Risk-averse: fixed price and clear scope wins
- Practical: demos and before/after > slide decks
- Slow winter (Nov–Dec), strong Jan–Mar and Sep–Oct

### Buyer personas

**Persona A — Der überarbeitete Inhaber (Owner)**

- Age 40–58, built business over 20 years
- Works in field + does admin evenings/weekends
- Skeptical of "IT projects" after bad experiences
- Buys when: clear ROI, fixed price, local trust, referral
- Quote: *"Ich hab keine Zeit für sowas — aber ich hab auch keine Zeit mehr für Excel."*

**Persona B — Die überforderte Bürokraft (Office manager)**

- Handles quotes, scheduling, invoicing, phone
- Knows exactly where processes break
- Often internal champion who convinces owner
- Buys when: you make her job easier, not threaten it
- Quote: *"Jeden Montag brauche ich 4 Stunden nur für die Auswertung."*

**Persona C — Der externe Steuerberater (Channel partner)**

- Sees messy data from many Handwerk clients
- Won't implement, but will refer trusted fixers
- Buys when: you don't compete, you make their job easier
- Quote: *"Die schicken mir Belege per WhatsApp."*

### Anti-personas (say no)

| Type | Why |
|------|-----|
| < 5 employees | Can't afford €4.5k; DIY mindset |
| > 80 employees | Want ERP integrators; long procurement |
| "We need an app" | Custom dev; out of scope |
| No budget, want free POC | Time waster |
| Chaotic owner, no time for calls | Delivery will fail |

## 10. Sales process

### Funnel metrics (targets, mature)

| Stage | Conversion | Volume/quarter |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| Outreach/conversations | — | 40 |
| Discovery calls | 25% | 10 |
| Proposals sent | 50% | 5 |
| Closed won | 40% | 2 Sprints |

### Discovery call script (30 min)

**Minutes 0–5: Rapport**  
- How long in business? How many employees? What trade?

**Minutes 5–15: Pain (let them talk)**  
- Walk me through last Monday morning in the office.  
- What takes the most time every week?  
- Where do mistakes happen?  
- What happens when [Bürokraft] is sick?

**Minutes 15–22: Quantify**  
- How many hours/week on this process?  
- What does that cost in salary or overtime?  
- What happens when invoices are late?

**Minutes 22–28: Solution fit**  
- Explain Sprint with their specific process  
- Show demo or case study  
- Fixed price, 14 days, exactly what's included

**Minutes 28–30: Next step**  
- Send proposal within 48h  
- Or offer Audit if not ready for Sprint

### Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | ROI calc: 8h/week × €30/h = €1,240/month saved |
| "We tried software before" | "This isn't ERP — one process, 14 days, fixed price" |
| "No time for this" | "You invest 3 hours total; I do the rest" |
| "My nephew does Excel" | "Great for fixes — I build systems that run daily without you" |
| "Need to think" | "Sure — proposal valid 7 days; Audit is lower risk entry" |

### Proposal structure (1–2 pages)

1. Your understanding of their problem  
2. Proposed Sprint type (A/B/C/D/E)  
3. Deliverables checklist  
4. Timeline (14 days from kickoff)  
5. Fixed price + payment terms  
6. What's excluded  
7. Next step + signature
