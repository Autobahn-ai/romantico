## 1. Executive summary

### The business

A remote consulting practice that **automates manual office processes** for US (and UK) **home service companies** — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping — with 10–50 employees, using **low/no-code tools** (n8n, Google Sheets, Looker Studio).

### The problem

Tens of thousands of profitable trade businesses still run office work through **Excel, text messages, paper, and phone calls**. Same pains as German Handwerk:

- 5–15 hours/week of duplicate data entry
- Late invoicing and cash-flow gaps
- No visibility on job profitability
- Owner burnout (Sunday paperwork)
- Office manager frustration

They don't buy enterprise software because it's expensive and slow. Nobody offers a **fixed-price, 14-day, one-process fix** in plain English.

### The solution

**Productized Automation Sprints** — one process, 14 days, fixed price, English documentation, Loom handover video. Upsell **Care Plan** retainers.

## 4. Market analysis

### Market size (USA)

- **~2+ million** home service businesses (broad)
- Your slice: **10–50 employees**, still on Excel/texts for key ops
- Serviceable remotely: **unlimited geography** (start with 2–3 states for marketing focus)

**Suggested initial geo focus (pick one):**

- Texas, Florida, Georgia (huge HVAC/plumbing, business-friendly)
- Or: Ohio, Pennsylvania, North Carolina (dense trades)

Don't need to travel — need to be **reachable on Zoom**.

### Industry trends

1. **Technician shortage** — office staff stretched thin  
2. **Private equity roll-ups** — many shops still messy pre-acquisition  
3. **ServiceTitan fatigue** — expensive; many never fully adopt  
4. **"AI automation" hype** — owners want results, not buzzwords  

### Competition

| Competitor | Weakness | Your edge |
|------------|----------|-----------|
| ServiceTitan / Jobber / Housecall Pro | Heavy, expensive, long onboarding | One process in 14 days |
| Local IT | Break-fix, not process | Ops-first |
| Zapier freelancers | Generic | Trades-specific language |
| Offshore VA | Cheap labor | System design + reliability |

**Saturation:** Generic automation is crowded; **"14-day HVAC office sprint"** is not.

### UK secondary market

- Same model, GBP pricing (£3,500–6,500)
- Smaller TAM, less US competition
- Add in year 2 if US pipeline is full

- **Price:** $1,200 → $2,000 (mature)  
- **Deliverables:** Process map, top 3 problems, 90-day roadmap, Sprint quote

### Automation Sprint — core offer

**14 days, ONE process, fixed price.**

| Sprint | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **A. Timesheet capture** | Texts/calls/paper | Form → Sheet → weekly payroll export |
| **B. Quote follow-up** | Quotes die in Excel | Pipeline + auto reminders day 3, 7, 14 |
| **C. Job dashboard** | No overview | Live dashboard from existing data |
| **D. Invoice reminders** | Late billing | Job-complete → checklist + alert |
| **E. Parts reorder** | Ad-hoc | Low-stock form → supplier email |

**Every Sprint includes:**

- Kickoff (60 min Zoom)
- Before/after process diagram
- 1–2 n8n workflows
- Sheet/Airtable structure if needed
- Looker mini-dashboard (if applicable)
- 15-min Loom handover (English)
- 1-page user guide (English)
- 14 days bugfix support

**Excluded:** New ServiceTitan rollout, custom apps, accounting advice, unlimited meetings.

## 13. Legal & compliance

> Not legal advice — consult a professional for your country of residence.

### If you live in Germany/EU, serve US clients

- Register freelance business locally (Freiberufler/Gewerbe)  
- US clients = **export of services** (B2B)  
- Invoice in USD; use Wise/PayPal/Stripe for receipts  
- VAT: typically **not German VAT on US B2B exports** — confirm with Steuerberater  
- **DSGVO/GDPR** still applies to how YOU store EU-hosted data about US client employees  
- AVV/DPA with clients recommended  
- Professional indemnity insurance (EU insurer OK)  

### If you live in the US

- LLC (recommended)  
- E&O insurance  
- Sales tax: usually not on consulting services (state-dependent)  
- 1099-friendly invoicing  

### Client data

- US employee names, job times, customer addresses — treat as confidential  
- EU-hosted VPS is fine with proper DPA  
- Don't put client data in ChatGPT without consent
