# Discovery Call Script — Home Services Automation Sprint

**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Language:** English  
**Platform:** Zoom  
**Goal:** Qualify pain, quantify ROI, send proposal or offer Audit

---

## Before the call

- [ ] Google the company (trade, city, size, reviews)
- [ ] Check their website — do they mention fleet size, services?
- [ ] Pick likely Sprint type (A–E)
- [ ] Calendly ready for follow-up
- [ ] Demo or case study link ready

**Time zone:** Confirm you're calling US Eastern/Central morning if client is US.

---

## Opening (2 min)

> "Hi [Name], thanks for making time. I help HVAC and plumbing companies with about 10 to 50 employees automate office work that's still stuck in Excel and text messages — things like timesheets or quote follow-up. Fixed 14-day projects, flat fee.  
>  
> Today I'd like to understand how your office runs and see if I can actually help. Not a hard sales call — more of a fit check. Sound good?"

---

## Their world (12 min) — LISTEN

1. "Tell me about the company — what trades, how many people, how many trucks?"

2. "Walk me through a job from first call to getting paid."

3. "What does a typical Monday morning look like in the office?"

4. "Where do you lose the most time on manual work?"

5. "How do techs submit hours or job notes today?"

6. "What software are you paying for? What's actually getting used?"

7. "What happens when your office manager is out sick?"

8. "Where do mistakes cost you money?"

**Notes:**

```text
Trade:           _______________
Employees:       _______________
Tools today:     _______________
#1 pain:         _______________
Hours/week:      _______________
Decision maker:  _______________
```

---

## Quantify (8 min)

1. "Roughly how many hours per week does [process] take?"

2. "If we multiply that by what you pay office staff — ballpark $25 to $35 an hour — what does that cost monthly?"

3. "What happens when invoices go out late?"

4. "Have you tried to fix this before? What happened?"

**ROI (say aloud if relevant):**

> "Eight hours a week at thirty dollars an hour is almost a thousand a month. Our sprint is fifty-five hundred once. It pays for itself in the first month if we fix this."

---

## Solution fit (5 min)

> "Based on what you described, this sounds like our [Timesheet / Quote follow-up / Dashboard] Sprint.  
>  
> In 14 days we build [describe in their words]. You get a written guide, a video walkthrough, and two weeks of support. Flat fee: [five thousand five hundred] dollars. Your team's time is about five hours total."

**If not ready:**

> "If you want a lower-risk start, our Office Process Check is twelve hundred dollars — one week, you get a roadmap and a fixed quote for the sprint."

---

## Close (3 min)

**Good fit:**

> "I'll send a one-page proposal by [day]. If it looks right, we pick a start date. Any questions?"

**Always ask:**

> "Do you know another contractor owner dealing with similar office chaos?"

---

## Disqualify politely

- Wants custom mobile app  
- Under 8 employees  
- No decision maker on call  
- Wants free proof of concept with no commitment  
- Full ServiceTitan replacement in 2 weeks  

> "That sounds like a bigger software project than what I do. I can refer you to an implementer, or we could start with one small process audit."

---

## After call (48h)

- [ ] Summary email  
- [ ] Proposal PDF if qualified  
- [ ] CRM log  
- [ ] Follow-up reminder day 7
