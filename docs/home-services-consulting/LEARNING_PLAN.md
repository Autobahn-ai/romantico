## 1. Learning philosophy

### What changed from the Handwerk plan

| Removed | Added |
|---------|-------|
| German vocabulary | US trades terminology |
| ToolTime / plancraft | ServiceTitan / Jobber demos |
| Steuerberater channel | CPA / bookkeeper channel |
| Innung events | PHCC, ACCA, Facebook groups |
| German proposals | English templates |

### Principles (unchanged)

1. Learn by building portfolio demos  
2. Sell after 16 weeks — real clients teach faster  
3. One tool deep (n8n) before extras  
4. **Industry language matters** — say "work order" not "workflow entity"  
5. Templates beat improvisation  

### You do NOT need

- German  
- Coding  
- ServiceTitan certification  
- US work visa (remote services export)

## 9. US trades domain curriculum

### 8-week topic plan

| Week | Topic | Resource |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | HVAC business model (service vs install) | YouTube |
| 2 | Quote → dispatch → job → invoice flow | Podcasts |
| 3 | Field software landscape | ServiceTitan demo |
| 4 | Office roles: owner, CSR, dispatcher | LinkedIn profiles |
| 5 | Seasonality (AC summer, heating winter) | Industry articles |
| 6 | PE roll-ups and ops chaos | Trade press |
| 7 | CPA/bookkeeper view of contractor books | Interview 1 CPA |
| 8 | Facebook group lurk — real owner language | Save phrases |

### Vocabulary (learn by month 3)

| Term | Context |
|------|---------|
| CSR | Customer service rep (phones) |
| Dispatcher | Schedules techs |
| Tech / technician | Field worker |
| Install vs service call | Job types |
| Estimate | Quote |
| Work order | Job ticket |
| Flat rate | Fixed price repair |
| T&M | Time and materials |
| Callback | Return visit |
| AR aging | Unpaid invoices |
| Job costing | Profit per job |
| Truck stock | Parts on van |

### Phrases owners actually use (for sales)

- "My office manager is drowning."  
- "We live in Excel."  
- "Techs text us hours and half get lost."  
- "I find out we're losing money on jobs too late."  
- "We paid for [software] and nobody uses it right."  

Use their words in proposals.

## 10. Consulting skills

| Skill | Resource | When |
|-------|----------|------|
| Discovery | "The Mom Test" (English) | Month 2 |
| Pricing confidence | ROI calculator spreadsheet | Month 4 |
| LinkedIn outbound | 10 messages/week habit | Month 3+ |
| CPA networking | 1 CPA coffee/month | Month 4+ |
| Writing case studies | After each Sprint | Ongoing |

### English-only client communication standards

- All emails: clear subject, bullet points, no jargon  
- Loom videos: slow, show clicks, summarize at end  
- User guides: max 1 page, screenshots, "if X then Y"  
- Avoid: "workflow", "node", "API" — use "automation", "connection", "system"
