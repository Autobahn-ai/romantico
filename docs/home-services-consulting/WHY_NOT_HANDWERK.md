# Why We Pivoted Away From German Handwerk

## The constraint

German Handwerk businesses (10–40 employees):

- Conduct **all business in German** (calls, email, docs, field crews)
- Trust **local, German-speaking** partners
- Use German-specific software (ToolTime, plancraft, DATEV ecosystem)
- Buy through **Innung, Handwerkskammer, Steuerberater** — all German

**If you don't speak German fluently, this niche is the wrong fit.** You would lose deals at the first call, and delivery (user guides, training Bürokraft) would fail.

## What we kept

| Element | Unchanged |
|---------|-----------|
| Business model | Ops Audit → 14-day Sprint → Care Plan |
| Tools | n8n, Google Sheets, Looker Studio |
| Target size | 10–50 employees, low digital maturity |
| Core pains | Excel, texts, paper, late invoices, no job visibility |
| Timeline | 20h → 40h → quit → 60h over 24 months |

## What we changed

| Element | Old | New |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Market | Germany | **USA (primary), UK (secondary)** |
| Language | German | **English** |
| Industry label | Handwerk | **Home services / trades** |
| Delivery | Local possible | **100% remote** |
| Pricing | €4,500–7,500 | **$5,000–8,500 USD** |
| Referral channel | Steuerberater | **CPA / bookkeeper** |
| Trade associations | Innung | **PHCC, ACCA, NECA, BNI** |

## Why US home services is the best English alternative

| Criterion | Score | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| Easy to learn | ★★★★★ | Same processes as Handwerk, tons of English content |
| Clients value the offer | ★★★★★ | Identical Excel/text chaos pain |
| Not saturated (your offer) | ★★★★☆ | Many marketing agencies; few **14-day fixed Sprint** specialists |
| No German needed | ★★★★★ | All client work in English |
| Remote-friendly | ★★★★★ | Zoom + Loom is normal in US SMB |
| Higher prices | ★★★★★ | US SMBs often pay **more** than German Handwerk for ops fixes |

## Other English options (ranked)

| Niche | Verdict |
|-------|---------|
| **US home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical)** | ✅ **Best pick** |
| **UK trades (plumbers, electricians, builders)** | ✅ Good; smaller market, GBP pricing |

## If you ever learn German

You can add Germany as a **second market in year 3+**. Don't start there without the language.
